Who We Are

CyberSafe is a cyber security awareness learning platform designed to educate individuals about online threats and responsible internet usage.

The platform provides educational lessons, quizzes, safety tips, and learning progress features to help users develop safer digital habits.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve cyber security awareness by providing accessible and understandable educational resources that help people protect themselves online.

Our Vision

Our vision is a safer digital environment where individuals have the knowledge and skills to identify online threats and protect their personal information.

Our Objectives

  • Educate users about common cyber security threats.
  • Promote safe internet browsing habits.
  • Teach users how to recognise phishing and online scams.
  • Encourage strong passwords and account protection.
  • Provide quizzes to assess users' understanding.
  • Promote responsible use of digital technology.

What CyberSafe Offers

  • Cyber security learning modules.
  • Interactive knowledge assessment quizzes.
  • Personal learning progress tracking.
  • Cyber security awareness tips.
  • Achievement certificates for successful learners.
