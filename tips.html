Cybersecurity Safety Tips

Discover practical ways to protect your personal information, devices and online accounts.

💡 Cybersecurity Tip of the Day

Use strong and unique passwords for all your online accounts.

Important Safety Recommendations

🔐

Password Security

Use long, unique passwords for different accounts. Avoid sharing your passwords with anyone. Enable multi-factor authentication whenever possible.

🎣

Email Protection

Avoid opening suspicious email attachments. Verify unexpected messages and never enter your password through untrusted links.

📱

Mobile Phone Security

Protect your phone with a strong screen lock. Install applications from trusted sources and keep your operating system updated.

🌐

Safe Browsing

Check website addresses before entering personal information. Avoid suspicious websites and unnecessary downloads.

👥

Social Media Safety

Review privacy settings, avoid oversharing personal information and be careful when accepting requests from unfamiliar accounts.

🏦

Online Banking Safety

Never share your banking PIN or verification codes. Use official banking applications and report suspicious transactions immediately.

📶

Wi-Fi Protection

Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted public networks. Secure your home Wi-Fi with a strong password and modern encryption.

🦠

Malware Prevention

Install trusted security software, update your applications and avoid downloading unknown files. Back up important information regularly.

🕵️

Protect Your Identity

Keep sensitive information private. Do not unnecessarily share your home address, financial details or identification documents online.

🚨

Online Scam Prevention

Be suspicious of unexpected prizes, urgent payment requests and offers that appear too good to be true. Always verify before making payments.

🔄

Software Updates

Install operating system and application updates promptly. Updates often contain important security fixes.

💾

Data Backup

Keep backup copies of important documents and photos in secure locations. Test your backups occasionally.
