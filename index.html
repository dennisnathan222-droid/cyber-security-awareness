Stay Safe in the Digital World

Welcome to CyberSafe, a cybersecurity awareness and training platform designed to help students identify online threats, protect personal information and develop safer digital habits.

Start Learning

About Our Platform

CyberSafe promotes cybersecurity awareness through educational resources and interactive assessments. Learn how to recognize threats and make informed decisions when using the internet.

Cybersecurity Training Modules

🎣

Phishing Awareness

Learn to recognize suspicious emails, fake websites, unexpected links and messages that attempt to steal personal information.

🔐

Password Security

Create long, unique passwords for different accounts. Use a password manager and enable multi-factor authentication where possible.

🦠

Malware Awareness

Understand viruses, spyware and ransomware. Avoid suspicious downloads and keep your devices and applications updated.

📱

Social Media Safety

Protect your privacy, review account settings and think carefully before sharing personal information or clicking unknown links.

📶

Safe Internet Use

Use trusted networks, secure websites and updated software. Avoid sensitive transactions on untrusted devices or networks.

🚨

Online Fraud Prevention

Recognize suspicious payment requests, fake giveaways and impersonation scams. Verify requests through trusted channels.

Quick Safety Tips

✅ Never share your password or verification codes.

✅ Verify links before entering personal information.

✅ Enable multi-factor authentication.

✅ Install updates from trusted sources.

✅ Report suspicious activity to the appropriate authority.
